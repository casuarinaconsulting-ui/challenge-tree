## The narrative
Relief hook, then a fast colourful feature run, then it widens to the real collective impact, then the turn: ordinary people are already doing this, so the wealthiest and biggest polluters have no excuse. This flips the usual weakness of individual-action apps and turns the collective proof into the case for holding power accountable.

## Text and safe areas
- Use the exports in `tiktok-frames-1080x1920/` (13 frames, 1080x1920 with TikTok safe zones built in: clear of the Following/For You tabs at the top, the button rail on the right, and the caption area at the bottom). The `tiktok-frames/` folder is the raw full-bleed art if ever needed.
- Big, bold captions on a subtle dark band so they read on the light app screens too.
- Keep any extra text you add in CapCut out of the right edge (like and share buttons) and the bottom ~15% (username and caption).
- The badge unlock and the 1% chart are the two strongest beats. Lead the first 2 seconds with movement.

## Sound
- A calm or uplifting trending audio for reach (voiceless, so the text carries it).
- Save the rainforest sting for the final 2-second brand outro.

## Voice and honesty (non-negotiable)
- Voice is "we," not "I" (Casuarina is a we).
- Species badges are to "meet and learn about," never "protect." The app raises awareness, it does not fund conservation (yet).
- The 1% stat (the richest 1% out-emit the poorest 50%) is real and already appears in the app's own daily content.
- The app is free, no app store, works in any browser.
